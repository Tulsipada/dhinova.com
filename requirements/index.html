App Requirements

Checklist for product, design, technical, delivery, and compliance details.

  • Define the core problem

    Product

    Share the user problem, target audience, and success metrics for the app.

  • List must-have features

    Product

    Separate MVP features from later-phase ideas so we can scope delivery clearly.

  • Provide brand assets

    Design

    Logo, colors, fonts, and any existing UI references or competitor apps you like.

  • Platforms & integrations

    Technical

    iOS, Android, web - plus payments, maps, auth, CRM, analytics, or third-party APIs.

  • Data & admin needs

    Technical

    User roles, dashboards, content management, reporting, and storage expectations.

  • Timeline & budget range

    Delivery

    Target launch date, launch markets, and an approximate budget band for planning.

  • Security & compliance

    Compliance

    Any requirements for privacy, encryption, accessibility, or industry regulations.

  • Ownership & support

    Handoff

    Who owns accounts, app store publishing, and post-launch maintenance preferences.
